# HARDCORE v1.7 plan: frame powers and weapons in the campaign

Base: `hardcore_v1.6.html`.
- New **Special** button: C or Shift on keyboard, Y on a gamepad, a SP touch button; A+B together also works. Replaces the generic burst.
- **POWER meter** (0-100): fills by landing hits (+5) and beating robots (+12), starts at 50. A special costs 50 (knife throw 25).
- Every body has its own power (from the deck's Tools and Skills slide where it applies):
  core: knife throw | Basic: power glove rocket punch | Brute: hammer of might ground slam | Walker: shoulder laser beam | Titan: giant sword
  Scrapper: spin | Lancer: spear thrust | Hound: pounce | Shieldbot: shield bash | Brute robot: ground pound | Walker robot: rocket rain
- **Weapons**: crates can drop Sword of Justice, Hammer of Might, Shoulder Laser, Rocket Launch. Walking over one mounts it on your outer body; it replaces that body's power and stays with the body if you eject.
- HUD: power bar and the current special's name. English and Hebrew.
