# HARDCORE v1.11 plan: ticking heads, and beating bosses without jumping
Base: `hardcore_v1.10.html`.
- **Ticking heads**: ~1 in 3 popped heads is a bomb (lit fuse, flashing, ticking faster near the end, about 6 s). The blast hurts robots, the boss and you.
  A kicked ticking head explodes on contact.
- **Topple** (new boss state for the Colossus and Maker): a blast at its legs or fist, or a Chain Hook pull, topples it; its head lies on the floor in front for ~3.5 s and ground hits on it do double damage. No jump needed.
- **Chain Hook** weapon (special): fires a chain down your lane. Colossus/Maker: topple. Warden: pulled down. Robots: dragged to you, dazed (grab-ready). A crate at the start of each boss arena always holds one; it can also drop from other crates.
- Hints: "TICKING HEAD! KICK IT AWAY", "ITS HEAD IS DOWN: HIT IT". English and Hebrew.
