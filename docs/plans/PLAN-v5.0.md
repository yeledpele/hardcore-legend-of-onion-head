# HARDCORE v5.0 plan: flat colour shapes and simple animation
Base: `hardcore_v4.0.html` (all gameplay from gamedesign.md stays). Refs: Ben's deck slides (flat mint silhouettes on plum, white cut-out marks,
magenta connector lines and label boxes, teal ground slab, "\ | /" ticks) and the "Worm RPG" comic (black panels, flat pink worm with
stripes and a dot eye, curved motion strokes, chunky pixel lettering, "▸ start ◂", "GAME OVER").
## Language
- No outlines, no shading, no gradients: flat fills only, 6 colours: plum-black bg, mint (Onion Head + frames), pale mint core, soft pink robots
  (stripe segments, dot eyes), white (marks, eyes, motion), magenta (UI lines/boxes), a little yellow (power, lightning).
- Silhouettes from the deck: lids, blocky torsos, arm blocks with finger pegs, separate legs, white triangle/pentagon chest marks.
- The core as on the deck: square box, ring eye, three-line sprout spray with a magenta tip, side sticks.
- Matryoshka stays hexagonal (flat hex pieces).
## Animation
- Squash and stretch on jump/land, idle breathing, worm-style curved motion strokes behind moving bodies, "\ | /" ticks for wind-ups
  and surprise, ring + tick bursts for hits, eye blinks, sprout sway, wing flaps, wobbles.
## Screens
- Title like the comic: black panel, big pink chunky HARD / CORE, "▸ PLAY ◂". GAME OVER panel. Story on black panels with the "found buried"
  slab. Ending with the King silhouette, "KING OF ROBOTS / MONSTER KILLER".
- HUD: flat mini-cores, mint shell bar, magenta label box for the special with a power bar, pink boss bar, magenta item-card labels.
- Levels as flat silhouette strips: giant swords and slabs (Burial Waste), flat pines and moon (Pine Forest), stacks and smoke (Foundry).
